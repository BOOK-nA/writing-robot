Chalk Drawing Board

The green area is the robot’s whole board, 50 × 50 cm. The robot writes exactly where you draw, at the same size as the grid (1 small square = 1 cm). One drag without lifting is one stroke. The robot writes your strokes in the same order and direction that you drew them.
